gst-plugins-base 1.28.6 and gst-plugins-good 1.28.6 under LGPL-2.1,
and proxy-libintl 0.5 under the GNU Library GPL version 2.
Their complete upstream license notices are retained without alteration.

Aro imposes no restriction on modification for personal use or reverse
engineering of the linked hosts for debugging modifications to these libraries.
You may replace compatible dynamic libraries in codex-resources/voice/lib
and compatible plugins in codex-resources/voice/lib/gstreamer-1.0.
Keep the loader names and ABI compatible with the original library.
The upstream build, packaging and projection scripts are retained in the
matching Codex source archive identified by manifest.json.

Gemini CLI 0.61.0 uses keytar 7.10.6, which loads libsecret and its
GLib/GIO/GObject dependencies dynamically on supported Linux targets.
Aro imposes no restriction on modification for personal use or reverse
engineering of Gemini, keytar or Node for debugging modifications to these
libraries. You may supply ABI-compatible replacements through the dynamic
loader's library search path, including LD_LIBRARY_PATH. Preserve the
library names and exported interfaces required by the linked addon.
The matching library sources, upstream notices and build instructions must
be available alongside the target-specific image and its source materials.

This notice grants no exception to the upstream licenses. The release must
provide equivalent access to complete matching sources and build inputs.
Successful replacement/relinking verification is a separate release condition;
this preparation does not assert that a released image passed that check.
